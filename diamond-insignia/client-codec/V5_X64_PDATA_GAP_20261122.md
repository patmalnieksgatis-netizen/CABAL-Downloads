This failure came from the scanner assuming that every x64 direct-call target has a PE `.pdata` unwind function range. The exact verified caller instruction `0x21CC2A: call 0x21D938` remains unchanged; absence of a covering PDATA entry is not evidence the call target is invalid. This does **not** establish that `0x21D938` is a complete leaf function, a loader or a decoder.
